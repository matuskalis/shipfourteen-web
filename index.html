07 / Questions

The awkward ones first

Only one of these is a live client site. Why trust the process?

Because you can check the rest yourself. Three of them are redesigns of sites that are still live, and each entry links the site it would replace, so you can put them side by side and judge. Beyond that the risk is capped: half up front, half on launch. If the midpoint review is not what you wanted, you stop and pay only the first half.

What if fourteen days is not enough for my site?

Then we cut the first release down to what fits and the rest becomes a second sprint. A real site live in two weeks beats a perfect one still in review after two quarters.

Who writes the content?

I do, from the scoping call and whatever you already have. You review and correct the facts. If you would rather write it yourself, that works and the price drops.

Can we edit the site ourselves afterwards?

Yes. If you need to change text and images regularly we wire in a CMS during the build and you get a short handover walkthrough. If the site is static by nature we skip the CMS and skip its cost.

What does it actually cost?

It starts at 300 EUR and the final number depends on how many pages there are and how much of the content already exists. You get the scope and the fixed price in writing before day one, so the number never moves after we start.

We already have a site. Can you rebuild it?

That is most of the work here. We keep what already earns its place, restructure what does not, and move you off whatever is slowing the current site down.
