Questions

What if fourteen days is not enough for my site?
Then we cut the first release down to what fits, and the rest becomes a second sprint. A real site live in two weeks beats a perfect one still in review after two quarters.
Who writes the content?
I do, from the scoping call and whatever you already have. You review and correct the facts. If you would rather write it yourself, that works too and the price drops.
Can we edit the site ourselves afterwards?
Yes. If you need to change text and images regularly, we wire in a CMS during the build and you get a short handover walkthrough. If the site is static by nature, we skip the CMS and skip its cost.
What does it cost?
It depends on how many pages and how much of the content already exists, so I quote it after the call rather than posting a number that fits nobody. Half upfront, half on launch.
We already have a site. Can you rebuild it?
That is most of the work I do. We keep what already earns its place, restructure what does not, and move you off whatever is slowing the current site down.
